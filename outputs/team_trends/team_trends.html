How to read these. Expected goals count the quality of chances a team creates and allows. Over a season they predict goals better than goals themselves do, because finishing swings around a lot. The two gap charts show where results have run ahead of or behind the underlying play, and those gaps usually shrink. The caveat: 3 games is a very small sample. Fixtures have not evened out, so a club that has played three strong opponents will look worse than it is. Treat this as a prompt to look closer, not a verdict.
